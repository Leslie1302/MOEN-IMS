Methodology Note
Three measures are surfaced separately. Access rate (headline) is the population access-rate formula: the GSS March 2026 baseline plus verified meter connections and completed communities' connection targets × persons-per-connection, over the region's population. Community completion = % of communities marked Completed in the region (BoQ-driven). Material flow = quantity received ÷ contracted across BoQ lines.
