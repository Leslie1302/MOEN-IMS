MOEGT IEPS

The Integrated Energy Project System is the Ministry of Energy and Green Transition's platform for running electrification materials end-to-end — from a field request to a powered community — across the SHEP, Cost Sharing, and Streetlights programmes.

Our Mission

To accelerate electrification across Ghana through precise inventory control, authorized material releases, transparent reconciliation, and works progress that rolls all the way up to the national electricity-access rate.

IEPS bridges the gap between ministerial authorizations and field-level delivery. Every request is tied to a community and package, released only under a signed release letter, tracked to site, reconciled against the Bill of Quantities, and converted into measurable works progress — so every pole, conductor, transformer, and meter is accounted for from warehouse to commissioning.

What the System Does

Drawdown Guardrails

QR-verified release letters and approval memos — one per request, or one per bulk batch — keep releases within ministerial authorization.

Multi-Programme Routing

SHEP, Cost Sharing, and Streetlights, with the consignee auto-resolved — the project consultant for SHEP, the Member of Parliament for the others.

Live Stock Lifecycle

Soft-hold on request, deduction on release, addition on receipt — warehouse balances update on the dashboards as materials move.

BoQ Reconciliation

Received-vs-contract tracking per package and community, with over-issuance detection and a justification workflow.

Logistics Loop

Transporter assignment with shared waybills, status from loaded to in transit to delivered, and consultant-verified site receipts.

Progress & Access Rate

Five-stage works (HT → LV → transformer → meters → commissioning) scored against BoQ-seeded targets, feeding the Ghana map and the national access rate.

Programmes

SHEP

Self-Help Electrification — the core programme. Communities carry predetermined package numbers.

Consignee: Consultant

Cost Sharing

Beneficiary-supported electrification with auto-generated package numbers per request.

Consignee: Member of Parliament

Streetlights

Public lighting deployments with auto-generated packages and pole/lumen specifications.

Consignee: Member of Parliament
