MOEGT IEPS

The Integrated Energy Project System is the Ministry of Energy and Green Transition's platform for running electrification materials end-to-end — from a field request to a powered community.

Our Mission

To accelerate electrification across Ghana through precise inventory control, authorized material releases, transparent reconciliation, and works progress that rolls all the way up to the national electricity-access rate.

IEPS bridges the gap between ministerial authorizations and field-level delivery. Every request is tied to a community and package, released only under a signed release letter, tracked to site, reconciled against the Bill of Quantities, and converted into measurable works progress.
