Approval memo — margins

The approval memo is internal and prints on a plain sheet — no letterhead, no seal, just an underlined MEMORANDUM heading. It therefore has its own margins rather than the letterhead's insets, which are sized to clear artwork that isn't on this page.

Saved settings apply to the next document generated — re-generate any release whose documents you want re-laid out.
